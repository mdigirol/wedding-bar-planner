Assumptions

  • One drink = one wine pour, one 12 oz beer, or one cocktail with one base-spirit pour. The Espresso Martini adds coffee liqueur on top of the base pour.
  • Starting drinker rates (light 0.5, moderate 1.0, heavy 1.5 per hour) are planning defaults, not measured data. The starting mix works out to about 0.88 drinks per drinker per hour, inside the 0.75-1.0 band published guides give for lighter crowds.
  • Each spirit-class slider (vodka, gin, rum, tequila, whiskey drinks) uses one base-spirit pour per drink. Triple sec, both vermouths, and Campari are fixed at one 750ml each; the inventory checks that one bottle covers the worst case where every drink in the related classes used it (¾ oz triple sec, 1 oz sweet vermouth, ½ oz dry vermouth, 1 oz Campari).
  • Handles are used only where the remaining volume after full handles exceeds one 750ml. Keep spare bottles unopened and unchilled if you plan to return them, and confirm the store's return policy in writing; some states prohibit alcohol returns.
  • Two bartenders are recommended at this size (one per 50 guests is the common guideline).
